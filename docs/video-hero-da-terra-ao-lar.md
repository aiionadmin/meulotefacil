# Vídeo "Da terra ao lar" — prompts de geração
Ferramentas: imagens-chave no Midjourney/Flux/Imagen; vídeo no Kling 2.x / Runway Gen-4 / Veo 3 usando QUADRO INICIAL + QUADRO FINAL (a imagem-chave N inicia a etapa N, a N+1 termina). 3–5 versões por etapa. Montagem no DaVinci/Premiere com fusões de 6–12 frames, LUT única quente, upscale no Topaz, master 4K 24fps. Versão vertical 9:16 para o mobile. Legenda "Imagens meramente ilustrativas" em uso publicitário.

## Bloco de consistência (início de TODOS os prompts)
CONSISTENCY ANCHOR — same location in every shot: a 40-hectare residential subdivision site on the outskirts of Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil. Gently rolling terrain sloping down to a small creek lined with Atlantic Forest trees on the left side of the frame. A eucalyptus grove on the ridge at the top right. Sugarcane fields beyond the hills. A divided highway curving along the bottom right edge. Distant hazy Campinas skyline with mid-rise towers on the horizon. Deep red-orange latosol soil. Future street layout: one main boulevard entering from the highway at the bottom right, curving up through the center, six secondary curved streets following the contour lines, a central green area with a leisure club. CAMERA: DJI Inspire 3, Zenmuse X9 full-frame, 24mm, 4K, 24fps, 16:9, smooth gimbal, constant slow speed, no shake, single continuous drone path. STYLE: photorealistic, premium Brazilian real-estate commercial, natural colors, subtle teal-and-orange grade, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no close-up faces.

## Etapas
01 Terra virgem — Dawn. Untouched pasture land, native shrubs, cattle trails, morning mist rising from the creek valley. Camera at 150m, slow forward glide from the highway side. Birds crossing. First golden light on the ridge.
02 Topografia — Surveyors with total stations and GNSS rovers, a small survey drone hovering, white stakes with orange ribbons appearing one by one tracing the future street layout, faint glowing lines briefly outlining streets and lots then fading. 130m.
03 Limpeza — Time-lapse: vegetation removed in strips revealing red soil, riparian forest along the creek preserved and fenced with protection tape, site office container and fenced yard appear near the entrance. Dust in sunlight.
04 Terraplenagem — Fast time-lapse: yellow excavators, bulldozers, motor graders and dump trucks shape terraced platforms and carve red-earth roads exactly along the planned layout, compactor rollers. Camera lowers to 100m, starts slow 15° orbit right.
05 Drenagem e redes — Time-lapse: trenches along every street, large concrete stormwater pipes lowered by excavators, blue water and ochre sewer pipes laid, manholes and catch basins, retention basin near the creek, trenches backfilled.
06 Guias e calçadas — Time-lapse: slipform machines extrude continuous white concrete curbs and gutters along every curved street, sidewalks formed, layout crisp over the red earth.
07 Pavimentação — Time-lapse: asphalt pavers lay fresh black asphalt with light steam, rollers following, road markings painted, planted median on the boulevard.
08 Energia — Late afternoon time-lapse: slim LED street poles rise along every street, underground power and fiber, small substation near the entrance, streetlights flicker on in a test then off.
09 Portaria e lazer — Time-lapse: perimeter wall and security fence, contemporary wood-and-glass entrance gate and guardhouse, central club built: clubhouse, pool filling with turquoise water, tennis and multi-sport courts, playground, walking trail along the creek.
10 Lotes prontos — Time-lapse: lawn rolled across lots and green areas, young yellow and pink ipê trees along streets, palms at the entrance, lots demarcated with corner markers. Empty subdivision complete and clean. 70m, golden hour beginning.
11 Casas subindo — Time-lapse: houses rise lot by lot at different paces — foundations, ceramic block walls, scaffolding, roofs, finished façades. Contemporary Brazilian architecture: flat and hidden-tile roofs, white, sand and natural wood cladding, large glass panels, backyard pools filling. Camera at 40m, orbit reaches 40°.
12 Bairro vivo — Golden hour to dusk: fully occupied neighborhood, manicured gardens, mature blooming ipês, cars in driveways, children cycling, families walking dogs, people at the club pool, warm interior lights turning on house by house, streetlights glowing. Camera rises and pulls back to 150m revealing the glowing neighborhood under a purple-orange sky with Campinas city lights on the horizon, matching the first frame's composition.

## Prompt negativo (todas as etapas)
cuts, scene changes, different location, inconsistent street layout, roads changing shape, hills moving, buildings melting or warping, objects popping in and out unnaturally, flicker, jitter, camera shake, fisheye, fast zoom, cartoon, CGI or video-game look, oversaturated, text, readable signage, logos, watermark, subtitles, rain, snow, fog, deformed people, deformed vehicles, close-up faces.
